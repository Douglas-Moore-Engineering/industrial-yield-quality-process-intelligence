# Research and Standards Basis

The pre-Azure architecture is grounded in the following reference set already adopted by the baseline:

- NIST 2026 Roadmap on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning for Smart Manufacturing
- NIST Digital Twins for Advanced Manufacturing
- NIST Manufacturing Digital Twin Standards
- NIST Digital Thread for Smart Manufacturing
- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 — Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security
- NIST AI 100-1 — Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)
- Framework for a Digital Twin in Manufacturing
- NIST Digital Twin Core Conceptual Models and Services

Architecture implications include heterogeneous sensing/control integration, trusted industrial data, validated digital twins, digital-thread traceability, OT reliability/safety preservation, lifecycle AI governance, and composable rather than monolithic twin design.
