## Entry and acceptance

The [readiness register](readiness-register.csv) records proposed role ownership, evidence required, blockers and gate-relative dates. Role labels are planning assignments, not named or accepted responsibilities.

Before customer build commitment approve discovery/scope, decision authority, SOW-to-requirement/deliverable/acceptance traceability, responsibility/dependency matrix, deployment ownership and data/privacy/IP constraints. Establish standards applicability/tailoring before the site requirements baseline is frozen.

Then map source systems, identifiers/genealogy, feature extraction/time availability, process baseline, thresholds, IAM/SSO, network, retention/residency, capacity and SLO/RPO/RTO. Validate representative site data and shadow behavior before advisory canary. Record ORR separately from CAR; reference G2 does not substitute for either. Customer production activation requires applicable CAR disposition and technical release approval; contractual deviations require recorded authority, conditions and risk treatment.

## Ownership and service

Approve ownership for cloud/billing, DNS/certificates, identities/keys, repositories/CI/CD, registries/models, storage/data, logs/monitoring, backups/recovery, support, incident and release authority. No response or uptime commitments are implied.

Before customer reliance agree severity/escalation, support hours, monitoring ownership, training, administrator/operator handoff and known risks. Distinguish reusable background IP, customer configuration/trained artifacts, third-party licenses and customer data rights. Keep proprietary material in an authorized customer workspace.

Every engagement needs a supported operation, transfer, renewal or exit path. Exit must cover export/archive, identity/credential revocation, resource transfer/deletion, retention/backup expiry, subscription closure and customer confirmation. Do not mark handoff/deletion complete without evidence.
